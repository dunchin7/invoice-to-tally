## The ITC protection argument (this is the one that matters to partners)

Since the April 2025 GSTR-2B hard-locking, unmatched invoices = **permanent ITC loss**.
No appeal, no retrospective claiming.

The risk per firm depends on supplier filing compliance. Assume 3–5% of suppliers file late or
not at all in any given month. On ₹50L of monthly purchase value:

```
₹50L purchases × 18% GST = ₹9L potential ITC
3% non-filing rate        = ₹27,000 ITC at risk per month
                          = ₹3.2L per year — on a single client
```

Across 200 clients, even 1-in-50 slipping through = **₹64L/year of client ITC loss
that comes back to your firm as "why didn't you catch this?"**

We flag every GSTIN status mismatch and every amount discrepancy before you file GSTR-3B.
